SMS Alerts — Terms & Conditions

Last updated: October 14, 2026

Program description

NW Software Solutions System Alerts sends operational text messages about the health of the software systems we build and run — for example, a failed scheduled job, a service that stopped, or a database that needs attention. Messages are informational only. We never send marketing or promotional messages.

Who receives messages

Alerts go only to NW Software Solutions Inc. staff responsible for maintaining these systems, who have opted in.

How you opt in

A staff member opts in by providing their mobile number to NW Software Solutions Inc. in writing and explicitly agreeing to receive system alert text messages. Consent is not a condition of any purchase.

Message frequency

Message frequency varies with system activity, typically 0–10 messages per week.

Costs

Message and data rates may apply.

Opt out

Reply STOP to any message to unsubscribe. You will receive one confirmation message and no further alerts.

Help

Reply HELP for help, or email nafberger@gmail.com.

Sample message

"NW Software Alerts: Nightly backup job FAILED at 2:14 AM. Check the server. Reply STOP to opt out."

Carriers

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Privacy

See our Privacy Policy. Mobile information is never shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.
